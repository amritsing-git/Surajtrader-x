06 / ABOUT SURAJTRADERX

Built Around Structure.

SurajTraderX is an educational trading platform focused on helping learners understand NIFTY markets through a structured 15-minute framework — without tip-based promises or guaranteed-return claims.

THE APPROACH

Learn the process before the trade.

The course is designed around market structure, breakout conditions, risk management, backtesting and trading psychology. The goal is to give students a repeatable learning framework they can study, test and refine.

All material is provided for educational purposes. Market conditions can change and trading involves the risk of loss.
