PAYMENT SETUP
Open js/script.js and replace:
upiId:"YOUR-UPI@BANK"
with the client's VERIFIED UPI ID.

The payment page supports a selected-plan checkout flow, UPI deep link and QR generation after a real UPI ID is configured. Never collect a customer's UPI PIN, OTP or card password on the website.

The Contact page is a normal customer enquiry page. Submitting the form opens the visitor's email app addressed to surajjawale942@gmail.com. A backend/email service can be connected later for automatic server-side form delivery.
